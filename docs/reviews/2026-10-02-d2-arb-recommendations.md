# Architecture review — D2 recommendations: ADRs and Technology Radar

Status: **audit record; non-normative**.

## Scope

Decision D2 of the P0 exit review: whether the ARB accepts ADR-UIP-WKS-001 and
ADR-UIP-SEC-001, and where each Technology Radar entry the UI Platform owns
that is marked pending ARB should sit. `oklch` is covered separately (P0 exit
review, "D2 recommendation: `oklch`"). Every claim below was fetched from its
source on 2026-10-02 and checked against the page text. Each recommendation
applies the ring definitions of GDC-004 section 2.1 [1]:

- **Assessed:** "experimental or undergoing evaluation";
- **Trial:** "verified in pilot programs. It is recommended for new services,
  but existing services are exempt";
- **Adopted:** "the default mandatory baseline. Deviations require an approved
  exception waiver";
- **Hold:** "deprecated. New implementations are prohibited from adopting it".

These are recommendations. The ARB decides; nothing here records a vote.
